# Brief: consolidation into the deliverables

Read A/dispatch/COMMON.txt and A/00-mandate-and-rulings.md first. Your profile, id and deliverables are in your instructions. Inputs: everything under A/needs/, A/mechanisms/, A/duplication/, A/candidates/, A/challenge/. You consolidate; where the inputs disagree you keep the disagreement visible and say what would settle it. Take the challenge's major findings into account: do not carry forward a claim it found false. Every statement about the implementation keeps its source reference (copy it from the input; re-check the ones your conclusions rest on most).

Deliverable files, each at most 3,500 words unless stated, each leading with its conclusion:

- A/01-capability-and-responsibility-map.md (+ .csv): each capability from the needs; the mechanisms and code locations that carry it today; who owns each responsibility today and how many places share it; lines and concepts involved.
- A/02-essential-vs-accidental-complexity.md: per mechanism, the essential complexity of the need against what exists, with an honest rough proportion and the main sources of the excess (compensation for another mechanism, remnant, special case, duplication, compatibility, speculative generality). A summary table first. Answer plainly: how problematic is the present architecture.
- A/03-duplication-and-competing-mechanisms.md (+ .csv): the representative catalogue, merged from the cross-cutting hunt and the mechanism assessments, de-duplicated, grouped by pattern, ranked by cost.
- A/04-candidate-architecture.md (at most 5,000 words): one recommended candidate, built from the two candidates and the challenge, with the viable alternatives at each main fork and what decides between them. Essential capabilities distinguished from mechanisms. Where the two candidates agreed independently, say so. Do not average: where they differ, choose and give the reason, and present the other as the alternative.
- A/05-obstruction-map.md (+ .csv): each concrete present arrangement that impedes the recommended architecture: kind (duplicated rule, competing authority, hardcoded special case, bypassed extension mechanism, unnecessary abstraction, compatibility mechanism, shared state or implicit dependency, distributed responsibility), source references, representative example, which part of the target it blocks, what removes it, and the roadmap step that does so.
- A/06-capability-dispositions.md (+ .csv): for each capability and mechanism: disposition (retain, simplify, consolidate, replace, remove, investigate further), rationale, evidence, expected benefit, migration obstacles, risks, confidence, and the uncertainty split into decision-material and settle-by-experiment. Where assessor, steelman and challenge disagree, show all.
- A/07-remediation-roadmap.md: incremental steps that keep the product working throughout. Order, prerequisites, what each step deletes, how to verify it, how to reverse it, migration risks, and which need the architect's decision first. Start with deletions and reversible experiments available now. Mark steps that touch persistent data or public contracts and the stronger assurance they need. No work items are created; this is a proposal.
- A/08-constraints-and-acceptance-scenarios.md: the architectural constraints that would keep the resulting design from eroding again (each stated so that it could be checked, with what a check would look at), and acceptance scenarios in given/when/then form. Include constraints against the patterns found: no branching on a mission-type name outside its definition, one owner per domain rule, one authority per store, no mode without two real users, every compensating guard names what it compensates. Say what this implies for an architectural knowledge and assurance capability (Wayfinder), following from the needs found here (ruling R11).
- A/09-open-decisions.md: every decision that needs the human architect: the question, the options, what the evidence says, what it blocks, and the default the roadmap assumes if no ruling is given. Order by how much each unblocks.
