# Review brief: challenge of the synthesis

Read R/dispatch/REVIEW-COMMON.txt first. Profile: paula-patterns. Reviewer id: V.

You review the documents under R/synthesis/ (every .md and .csv directly in that folder), against the pre-registration and against the findings and raw evidence. You did not write them. Assume each contains at least one error and look for it.

1. Hypothesis verdicts (hypothesis-assessment.md, hypothesis-finding-map.csv). For each of H01 to H08, apply the criteria of section 6 of the pre-registration yourself, from the findings and their raw artefacts, before you read the synthesiser's verdict for that hypothesis. Write your verdict first, then compare. Check: was a criterion adapted after the fact; was an exploratory observation used to decide; was a causal claim made from deterioration evidence; was a trend drawn across the convergence; was "not assessable" turned into support or contradiction; were disagreements between paired workstreams (C and C2, G1 and G2, H06a and H06b, E1 and E3) averaged away; do the declared breaches and briefing gaps in coordinator-procedure-notes.txt change anything; is the decisive set complete. State where you overturn a verdict and why. Do the same for the experiment PX-01 and the acceptance scenarios.
2. The other documents (coupling, deviations, assurance gaps, components, decision timeline, dependency models, independence and contracts, execution matrix, quality attributes and invariants, knowledge model, open decisions). For each: the five statements that carry most weight, traced to finding ids and raw artefacts; statements with no trace; observations that the packet contains and the document left out (search R/synthesis/input/observations-index.csv for counter-examples); proposals presented as findings; recorded intent presented as observed fact, or the reverse; absence of a prospective component treated as a deficiency; a blocked build treated as a defect; desk assessment presented as verification; independence judged from repository separation alone.
3. Alternative explanations. For each supported or contradicted verdict, name the best competing explanation of the same measurements and what evidence would tell them apart.

Outputs:
- R/review/verdict-challenge.md (at most 3,500 words): per hypothesis, your verdict, the synthesiser's, agreement or overturn, reasons with finding ids.
- R/review/synthesis-challenge.md (at most 3,000 words): per document, findings graded major (changes a conclusion), minor, note.
- R/review/challenge-items.csv: id, document, statement, grade, reason, finding_ids.
