## Rules for every statement

- Keep four views apart and label each statement: recorded-intent, observed, approved-plan, proposed. A document describing the implementation is a document-claim and does not establish an observed fact by itself.
- Keep evidence kinds apart: executed, static-derived, history-derived, document-claim.
- Keep revision kinds apart: head, core-pin, released, historical. Say where the development architecture and the supported distribution differ.
- Keep pre-registered measures apart from exploratory observations. Exploratory observations never decide a verdict.
- Keep evidence of deterioration apart from evidence for a cause. Causal statements are at most "consistent with" or "not consistent with".
- No trend across the convergence merge 2554bd13ad. Agent involvement is not a proxy for supervision.
- Cite finding ids (F-<WS>-NNN) for every statement, and name the evidence file when you opened it. A statement with no finding id is marked as your own inference.
- Where two workstreams disagree (including C against C2, G1 against G2, H06a against H06b, E1 against E3), report both and do not average them away. Do not drop duplicates or inconclusive results silently.
- Rates with denominators. State scope and what was not covered.
- Use the ecosystem's own terms (see /home/user/spec-kitty/docs/context/). The domain object is a Mission. Name the sense of overloaded words (primary, merge, routing, residue, projection, runner, coordination).
- Kira, HUD, Operations Deck and similar belong to a prospective consumption context; their absence is not a deficiency. Paperclip is a comparison only. Desk assessment (H08, AS-10) is never presented as empirical verification.
