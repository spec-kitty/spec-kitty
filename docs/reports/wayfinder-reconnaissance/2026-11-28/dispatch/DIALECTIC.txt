# Brief for the dialectical review

You take part in a structured disagreement about what a read-only architectural reconnaissance of the Spec Kitty ecosystem has shown. It was commissioned by the human architect, who decides. The purpose is to keep substantive disagreements, counterevidence and unresolved questions visible. Nobody is asked to reach consensus, and you must not manufacture one. Your role is named in your instructions.

First read R/dispatch/REVIEW-COMMON.txt (R = /home/user/spec-kitty/docs/reports/wayfinder-reconnaissance/2026-11-28/) and apply its envelope, profile loading, rules and output form, with R/dialectic/ as your only output folder. Then read 00 to 05 in R, everything in R/synthesis/ (except input/ and sealed/, which you consult as needed), R/review/, R/evaluation/, the design brief (the PDF under /root/.claude/uploads/, proposals of an outside observer, in the proposed view), and findings and raw evidence wherever a point rests on them. Every argument cites finding ids or evidence files. An argument without evidence is marked as conjecture.

Role A (position paper R/dialectic/position-A.md, at most 2,500 words). Argue as strongly as the evidence honestly allows: the ecosystem's architecture has drifted from its recorded intent in ways its current assurance does not catch; the hypotheses the evidence supports matter in practice; an architectural knowledge and assurance repository along the lines of the design brief is warranted. Concede what you cannot defend.

Role B (position paper R/dialectic/position-B.md, at most 2,500 words). Argue as strongly as the evidence honestly allows: the recorded governance and existing gates are doing their job to the extent the evidence can show; observed departures are bounded, known or deliberate; the measures or samples do not carry the weight put on them; the preliminary design is premature, too large, or aimed at the wrong problem, and smaller or different responses fit the evidence better. Concede what you cannot defend.

Both roles: after your position paper you will be sent the other paper and asked for a rebuttal (R/dialectic/rebuttal-A.md or rebuttal-B.md, at most 1,200 words): answer its strongest three points, say which of your own points you now withdraw or narrow, and which disagreements remain and what evidence would settle each.

Role R, recorder (profile minutes-mahad), runs last and reads both papers and both rebuttals. Output R/dialectic/dialectic-record.md (at most 3,000 words) and R/dialectic/disagreements.csv (id, question, position_A, position_B, evidence_A, evidence_B, status: conceded by A, conceded by B, narrowed, open; what_would_settle_it; whose_decision). Record: points of agreement reached without pressure; each disagreement kept in both parties' own words; counterevidence each side could not answer; questions nobody could answer; which open points are matters of evidence and which are choices for the human architect. Do not decide between the positions, do not add a third position, do not soften either.
