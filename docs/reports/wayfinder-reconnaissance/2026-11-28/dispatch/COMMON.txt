## Four views, never mixed

- `recorded-intent`: what a charter, ADR or architecture document at a revision decided.
- `observed`: what the code, build or history shows.
- `approved-plan`: a recorded, approved future change.
- `proposed`: a proposal without recorded approval.

## Evidence kinds

| kind | meaning | may support |
|---|---|---|
| `executed` | you ran a command and kept its output | any view |
| `static-derived` | computed from source at a revision without running it | observed |
| `history-derived` | computed from version-control or tracker history | observed |
| `document-claim` | a document at a revision says something | recorded-intent, approved-plan, proposed only |

A document's description of the implementation (including CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, changelogs and lists of known residuals) is a `document-claim`. It never supports an `observed` finding by itself: verify against code or history, or file it as recorded intent.

## Independence dimensions

Whenever you assess whether a capability is independent of another, keep five dimensions apart: logical (one owner of the responsibility), contract (consumers use only an explicit versioned public interface), source (the implementation can change or be replaced without editing consumers), distribution (built, versioned, released, installed on its own), deployment (runs as its own process or unit). Repository separation, a service or a network hop does not establish independence; in-process composition does not preclude it.

### Discontinuities

These are handled by rule, not by judgement at analysis time.

- **Convergence.** The merge `2554bd13ad` (2026-09-05) joined two lines that ran in parallel from 2026-08-19. It is a structural discontinuity. No trend is drawn across it. Its effect is reported on its own as the difference between P3a, P3b and the merge result.
- **Bulk commits.** A commit touching more than 500 files is excluded from per-commit measures and listed.
- **Non-code commits.** A commit touching only `docs/`, `kitty-specs/` or `.kittify/` is excluded from code measures and counted separately.
- **Issue numbers.** `#N` references belong to four trackers over time. Each reference is qualified by line and date before it is followed.
- **Extracted repositories.** The charter repository has no core history, and Events imported some core history. Cross-repository change is joined by time, issue and pull request, never by file path. Imported commits are counted once.
- **Renames.** A rename map is built first (for example `doctrine` to `charter/offering`) and applied to every historical revision.

## Finding record

```yaml
finding_id: F-<WS>-001
workstream: <your workstream name>
hypotheses: []            # leave empty; filled later by the synthesis
classification: observation   # observation | interpretation | hypothesis | recommendation
view: observed            # recorded-intent | observed | approved-plan | proposed
prereg:
  measure: <measure id from your brief, or "exploratory">
  deviation: null
subject:
  component: <component>
  repository: spec-kitty/<repo>
  revision: "<full sha or released version>"
  revision_kind: head     # head | core-pin | released | historical
claim: "<specific, bounded, with the number and its denominator>"
evidence:
  - id: E1
    kind: static-derived  # executed | static-derived | history-derived | document-claim
    reference: "evidence/<WS>/<file>"   # or repo path:line at the revision for a document-claim
    method: "<exact reproduction command>"
    sha256: "<of the artefact>"
    exit_code: 0
    environment: "<tool versions>"
    observed_at: "<UTC>"
scope:
  covered: ""
  not_covered: ""
  population: null
  sample: null
  seed: null
interpretation: null      # null on observations
derived_from: []          # finding ids; required on interpretations and recommendations
counterevidence: []
disconfirmation:
  search: "<what you did to look for the opposite>"
  result: "<what you found, even if nothing>"
relations: []             # {type: supports|contradicts|duplicates|refines, target: F-...}
confidence: high          # high | medium | low
analyst:
  profile: ""
  model: ""
verification:
  status: unverified
  by: null
quality_attributes: []
candidate_invariants: []
open_questions: []
```

Rules:

- Every finding carries a disconfirmation search: what you did to find the opposite, and its result. An empty `counterevidence` without one is rejected.
- Rates, not bare counts: give the denominator.
- Mark every finding as carrying a measure from your brief, or as `exploratory`. Exploratory findings are welcome and are never presented as answers to a measure.
- If a measure cannot be taken as written, stop that measure, record a deviation (what, why, what you did instead) and carry it in each affected finding. Do not silently adapt a definition.
- Do not put secrets, tokens or e-mail addresses of people in any output.
