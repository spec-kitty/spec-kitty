# Workstream A1: recorded intent (components, decisions, authority)

Everything below is in the recorded-intent, approved-plan or proposed view. It rests on document-claim evidence, plus one executed diff of two documents. No code was read for findings, and the report gives no verdict on the architecture.

## Profile and directives applied

- **Profile `curator-carla`** (`spec-kitty agent profile show curator-carla`). I applied its role (curator: catalog, organize, classify, audit) and its directive 018 (doctrine versioning: every claim is tied to a dated record at a named revision). Its avoidance boundary (no architectural design, no product implementation) matches the envelope.
- **Conflict.** The profile's goal of "filling documentation gaps" conflicts with the envelope's ban on remediation. The envelope won: gaps are recorded, not filled.
- **Charter context, action `specify`.** I applied DIRECTIVE_044 (canonical sources), DIRECTIVE_048 (version governance), the Terminology Canon and the writing doctrine. In practice:
  - CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are used only as tagged secondary sources.
  - Records are cited at head revisions.
  - Overloaded senses are named. For example, "kernel" names core's `src/kernel` and also the charter repository's Java `kernel.domain`.

## Method and tools

- **Tools:** Python 3.13.18, git 2.43.0 (read-only `git show` and `git archive` of core `5c4fc5a7` into scratch), GNU grep 3.11, spec-kitty-cli 4.0.0rc9 (read-only).
- **Scripts:** kept as `evidence/A1/script-*.txt`.
- **Indexing.** All ADR and decision files of the five primary repositories, the planning `decisions/` folder and the planning v0.11 ADR set were indexed with heuristic extraction of date, status, supersession and amendment markers (`script-index-adrs.txt`).
- **Seeded sample.** 40 ADRs were drawn with `random.Random(20261128)`, stratified by repository and era (`script-sample40.txt`, `deep-read-sample40.csv`). The frame is 465 records, excluding the 56 non-binding v0.11 proposals.
- **Purposive set.** 49 further ADRs that change ownership, location or contract of a component were picked by hand and tagged `purposive`. I read their status, decision and amendment sections (`deep-read-notes.csv`, 89 rows).
- **Measure ids.** M1 to M7 correspond to deliverables 1 to 7 of brief A1.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **Measure ids.** The brief names deliverables, not measure ids. I assigned M1 to M7 in deliverable order and use them in every finding.
2. **Independence values.** The brief allows claimed, explicitly-not-pursued and silent. I added `contested` for 3 cells where two records disagree, and counted it separately (F-A1-029).
3. **Relation vocabulary.** The brief lists five values. I added `must-not-ship` and `oracle-parity`, plus qualified variants written in parentheses, where a recorded rule did not fit those five (F-A1-031).
4. **Undated records.** 98 records (doc-kitty and v0.11) carry no date. I used the git first-add date at head, labelled `git-first-added(history-derived)` in `date_source`.
5. **Heuristic extraction.** Status class and amendment detection are heuristic.
   - Amendment counts are lower bounds: "Annotation" sections and inline "Superseded" banners are not counted.
   - The amendment regex was widened once, to include "Amended <date>" markers, and all counts were recomputed.
6. **Sample allocation.** The brief says "stratified by repo and era" without an allocation. I used roughly proportional allocation with a minimum per stratum (`deep-read-sample40.csv`, columns `stratum_population` and `stratum_sample`). The v0.11 proposals are outside the sampling frame.
7. **Head checkout.** Core's working checkout is three report commits ahead of `5c4fc5a7`. All core reads used `git archive` or `git show` of `5c4fc5a7`.
