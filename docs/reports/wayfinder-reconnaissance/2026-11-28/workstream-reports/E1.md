# Workstream E1: assurance inventory, exceptions, and the research-mission experiment

## Profile and directives applied

Profile `reviewer-renata` (role reviewer; canonical verbs review, flag, assess). Charter context loaded with
`spec-kitty charter context --action review --json`.

- Applied from the profile: its avoidance boundary (no implementation, no product decisions), which matches
  the brief's envelope; `DIRECTIVE_001` (component boundaries) and `DIRECTIVE_030` (quality gates) as the
  lens for the gate inventory; `DIRECTIVE_024` (locality) for keeping all writes inside the report folder.
- Applied from the charter: `NO_FULL_HEAVY_SUITES_IN_MISSION` (no test suite was run at all) and the
  Terminology Canon.
- Conflict: the profile's handoff of feedback to an implementer is not used. The envelope wins: nothing is
  proposed for remediation.

## Method and tools

git 2.43.0, Python 3.13.18 with PyYAML, gh 2.89.0 (read-only API calls), spec-kitty-cli 4.0.0rc9 (the
editable install; its source equals core `head`). All analysis ran on `git clone --shared` copies of the five
primary repositories at the `head` revisions of the common brief. Three scripts did the counting; they are
in `evidence/E1` as `script-*.txt`:

- `script-gates_all.txt` builds the gate inventory. One row per `tests/architectural/**/test_*.py` file in
  core is generated; the rows for other checks and other repositories are curated by hand from workflow
  files.
- `script-ledgers.txt` runs the ledger census: per ledger file it walks first-parent history and compares
  entry sets; inline markers are counted with `git grep` at two endpoints and dated with `git blame`.
- `script-px01-run.txt` logs every command of the experiment.

## Coverage

Covered: all nine deliverables. Not covered: organisation-level rulesets and environment rules; the
supplementary repositories' own CI; in-code allowlists entry by entry (counted through their caps, except
the two layer-rule ledgers); the orchestrator-api accept path; the three research steps after the stall.

## PX-01 in brief

The Mission was created with the documented command, the spec and plan were authored as the commands
asked, and `spec-kitty next` advanced through scoping and methodology. At `gathering` it returned `blocked`
(exit 1) with two guard failures. The guard waits for three `source_documented` rows in
`mission-events.jsonl`; no command in the source tree writes such a row, and the step prompt points to "your
harness's event-emission mechanism" without naming one. The guard also looks for `source-register.csv` in
the Mission directory, while the prompt and templates name `research/source-register.csv`. No workaround
was attempted. Log: `evidence/E1/px01-command-log.txt`; summary: `px01-summary.txt`.

## Deviations

| What | Why | What I did instead |
|---|---|---|
| M05b gross additions and removals are not the headline | 30 of 57 ledgers were created inside the range, often by moving a list out of code; their first fill would count as additions | Net change is split into ledgers existing at the start and ledgers created later; gross numbers stay in `ledgers.csv` |
| M05d age is per ledger file path | No rename map for ledger files was built | Ages are reported as lower bounds, confidence low |
| In-code allowlists are not entry-level | Reading 20 Python constants across history was out of proportion | Their size and history come from `_baselines.yaml`; two layer-rule ledgers were parsed entry by entry |
| "Where it runs" for curated rows has job-level line references | The inventory has 171 curated rows | Generated rows carry registry and router lines per file; curated rows cite the job |
| Sample of 30 ledger entries | A simple random sample puts 18 of 30 in one formatter list | Reported as drawn, plus a second seeded sample of 30 outside the two lint baselines, labelled secondary |
| Chain sample is stratified 6 + 6 | Two frames of different kind (charter list items, directives) | Both frames and draws are in `chain-sample.json` |
| PX-01 needed `sync_packs` in the clone | A fresh clone has no pack trees; the first prompt reported the internal pack missing | Ran the documented once-per-checkout step, logged as command 4 |
| `charter context` was also run for five more actions in the PX-01 clone | Needed for the chain | No state in `/home/user` was touched |
