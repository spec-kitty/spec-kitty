# One pin, read by every check that needs the charter:
#   - scripts/ci/validate_pack_schemas.py fetches the pack schemas (contracts/packs/) at `commit`;
#   - the "Charter service validation" job builds, or downloads, the charter service for the same
#     `commit` and asks it to compose and register every pack (scripts/ci/charter_service_check.py).
#   - the same job gates every pack on the charter's whole-pack check, pack-check (each pack, the closure and the
#     round trip; #252): the release's pack-check jar on a release pin, built from `commit` on a main pin
#     (scripts/ci/charter_pack_check_jar.sh, scripts/ci/charter_pack_check.py).
#
# Pin policy (operator ruling, 2026-11-18): pin a charter release or pre-release tag by default; pin
