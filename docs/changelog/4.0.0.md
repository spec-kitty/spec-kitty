# 4.0.0 — Hosted collaboration & the open-core structural finish

**Milestone:** [`4.0.0 release scope`](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/milestone/11) (open; the GA gate) ·
**Status:** active (release-candidate stage; `4.0.0rc5` published 2026-10-02, `4.0.0rc6` open on `main`) · **Declared:** 2026-09-04 · **Re-anchored:** 2026-10-06 ·
**Supersedes** the retired [`3.3.x`](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/milestone/5)
declaration for the hosted/experience work · **Originally advanced** (see [Where the goals stand](#where-the-goals-stand-2026-10-06) for today's state): epics #1800 (SaaS sync &
event-envelope hardening), #1091 (Team Kitty launch gate), #3322 (CLI auth & token-lifecycle
reliability), #2173 (infra-to-logic ports), #2519 (charter authoring & lifecycle), #3549
(event-log integrity).

> **Direction amendment (2026-10-01).** Team Kitty / SaaS work is frozen while the
> commercial vision is reviewed, so **G1 (hosted collaboration) is off the critical path**:
> nothing hosted gates 4.0.0 GA, which is the
> [4.0.0 release scope](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/milestone/11) milestone alone.
> External interaction flows through the CLI's adapter module (the status fan-out, the seam
> for a publish/subscribe system); producing events and draining them are separate concerns
> with separate toggles; and the charter may ship as its own package once `specify_cli`
> reaches it only through a stable API (#645). The G1 text below is kept as the record of
> the original intent. The operator detail is in the
> [4.0.0 Milestone Roadmap](../plans/4-0-0-milestone-roadmap.md#direction-update-2026-10-01-hosted-collaboration-is-off-the-critical-path).

## Where the goals stand (2026-10-06)

The goals below are the record of what was declared on 2026-09-04. What 4.0.0 GA now means:

- **GA is local CLI integrity.** The CLI never reports success while it loses, overwrites or
  strands local work. The [4.0.0 release scope](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/milestone/11)
  milestone (14 open, 210 closed, due 2026-10-16) is the whole gate; 119 of its 201 closed
  issues were P0.
- **G1 (hosted collaboration) is frozen.** Every G1 epic and P0 is closed or closed as not
  planned; the client-side successor epics #3892 and #3893 wait on 4.x Work. Since
  2026-10-06 Team Kitty is no longer actively supported, and its CLI entries are hidden
  from listings (ADR `2026-10-06-1`).
- **G2 is split.** #2173 (infrastructure ports) closed on 2026-09-27; #2519 (charter
  authoring) continues on CLI 4.x stable.
- **G3 (structural strangler) continues after GA** on the 4.x line: #645 (stable application
  API, with the Mission Status Read API as its read facet), #1619 (one execution context) and
  the pack-ecosystem epics. None of it gates GA.

The operator detail, including the open GA issues and the decisions still owed, is in the
[4.0.0 Milestone Roadmap](../plans/4-0-0-milestone-roadmap.md#release-posture).

## Why now

A **release-queue reconciliation on 2026-08-23** retired the `3.3.x` milestone (its open work
re-triaged into 4.0.0 or Product backlog), repurposed the former `3.2.x` milestone (#4) into
**Product backlog**, and split the near-term work into discrete **3.2.6** (stabilization,
code-complete), **3.2.7** (post-reconciliation follow-ups — doctrine-term renames,
profile-load consolidation, perf CI, dedups), and **4.0.0** (this cycle) milestones. 4.0.0
is the largest open milestone (80 open issues) and had **no declaration-of-intent**, breaking
the project's own [release-goals convention](release-goals.md) (one durable declaration per
milestone). This document is that declaration. The taxonomy re-anchor that surfaced the gap
is recorded in the milestone roadmap's
[2026-09-04 addendum](../plans/3-2-x-milestone-roadmap.md#addendum-2026-09-04--milestone-taxonomy-re-anchor-the-delayed-action-r).

## Theme

With 3.2.x's **core-domain single-sources-of-truth** substantially in place and 3.2.6 shipped
clean, 4.0.0 turns to **hosted collaboration and next-generation product capabilities** — the
hosted Team Kitty surface (sync, consent/identity, auth, launch) and the charter/runtime depth
that builds on the stabilized core. It is also the intended landing zone for the **open-core
structural strangler** that 3.2.x exists to finish — committed to this cycle by operator
decision (2026-09-04). Same discipline as the prior cycles: **no new shadow paths** — route or extract onto
an existing authority, never build a parallel one.

## Goals

### G1 — Hosted collaboration (Team Kitty)

*Off the critical path since 2026-10-01 (see the direction amendment above).*

Make the hosted surface a coherent, trustworthy product rather than a set of bolt-ons. The
durable invariants and sub-areas were held by the SaaS & Hosted Sync domain plan
(retired 2026-09-06, Convergence #3881 — the surface re-homed to the authoritative
upstream repos); this cycle ships their release-scoped work:

- **Sync & event-envelope integrity** — sync never reports success while authored state is
  stranded; every synced event carries a contract-valid envelope. Epic **#1800**; load-bearing
  P0 **#3278** (sync false success while `MissionCreated` stranded).
- **Consent & identity boundary** — only explicitly consenting projects transmit, under one
  unambiguous project→identity resolution. Load-bearing P0 **#3178** (wrong-authority egress);
  P1s **#3197 / #3196 / #3198**.
- **Auth & token lifecycle** — a logged-in CLI stays authenticated, human auth is
  browser-mediated, machine/CI auth has a supported path, refresh failures are named. Epic
  **#3322** (owns #3279 / #3277 / #3233).
- **Hosted rollout & launch gate** — fail-closed-by-default rollout, named readiness failures,
  launch defaults. Epic **#1091**.
- **Event-log integrity** — one canonical event store, honestly reported, delivered without
  silent loss. Epic **#3549**.

### G2 — Next-generation product capabilities

Deepen the governance and runtime surfaces the stabilized core now supports:

- **Charter authoring & lifecycle robustness** — the author/observe/reproduce paths, charter
  domain events, deterministic intake. Epic **#2519** (moved 3.2.x → 4.0.0 in the
  reconciliation; see the
  [Doctrine & Charter Domain Plan](../plans/domains/doctrine-charter-domain-plan.md) §3.1).
- **Infrastructure-to-logic separation** — inject ports for FS, Clock, GitOps, SaaSQueue,
  Renderer; keep the core pure and stub-testable. Epic **#2173** (moved 3.2.x → 4.0.0).

### G3 — Finish the open-core structural strangler

The G1/G2 strangler spine the 3.2.x cycle exists to finish — see
[Structural strangler spine — committed to 4.0.0](#structural-strangler-spine--committed-to-400-2026-09-04)
— is the structural foundation for 4.0.0's hosted and next-gen work. **Committed to 4.0.0 by
operator decision (2026-09-04):** the spine epics now carry milestone 4.0.0 on the live tracker.

## Success criteria

*Rewritten 2026-10-06; the 2026-09-04 placeholder criteria are listed below as the record.*

For 4.0.0 GA:

- [ ] The [4.0.0 release scope](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/milestone/11) milestone
      is closed, or each open issue is re-milestoned with a rationale.
- [ ] The nightly is green on the release commit; any `skip_nightly` waiver is an explicit,
      recorded operator decision.
- [ ] No command reports success while it loses, overwrites or strands local work (the
      consolidation, status and workflow P0s), with an explicit decision on #5780 and #5758.
- [ ] No new shadow path: no re-introduced sync transport, and only the adapter seam talks to
      an external system.

For the 4.x line after GA:

- [ ] The structural strangler spine lands or is explicitly re-dispositioned; no epic is left
      implicitly "cycle work" while off a release.
- [ ] #645 gives CLI, UI, MCP and SDK consumers one versioned surface, and `specify_cli`
      reaches the charter only through it.

Deferred with G1 (frozen 2026-10-01): hosted no-false-success and no-loss under load; the
consent/identity boundary; CLI auth across device-flow and machine paths (#3892); the Team
Kitty launch gate (closed as #1091).
