- **`move-task --reviewer` is an identity, and approvals refuse a non-claimant** (#7569, #7573). `--reviewer` now takes an identity such as `codex::reviewer-renata`, sets the approver when `--agent` is absent, and must match `--agent` and the review claimant; a free-text name such as `"Jane Doe"` now refuses. With no git-user fallback, an approval with no `--agent` and no review claim refuses with `REVIEW_NOT_INDEPENDENT`. Scripts may also hit `APPROVER_NOT_CLAIMANT` (an identity other than the claimant, or `--agent`, `--reviewer` and `--profile` disagreeing), `REVIEW_CYCLE_CITATION_MISSING`, `REVIEW_CYCLE_NOT_ADOPTABLE` and `REVIEW_SLOT_DAMAGED` (pass `--approval-ref`). Each exits 1 and writes nothing. Pass the claimant with `--agent`, or use `--force`, recorded as a forced move.
