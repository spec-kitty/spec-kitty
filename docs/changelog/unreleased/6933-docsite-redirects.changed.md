- **Every legacy DocFX URL keeps resolving on the doc-kitty docsite** (#6933, epic #6100). **Before:** the doc-kitty site built no redirects, so all 970 URLs of `site/url-baseline.txt` would have broken at cutover. **After:** `scripts/docs/site_redirects.py` generates `site/legacy-redirects.json` from the baseline, `site/moves.yaml` and the published set, and doc-kitty's `legacyHtmlRedirects` writes a redirect stub for each of its 929 keys. A moved page redirects to its new page, and a draft, archived or excluded page to its section hub. The `docs-site` gate fails when the committed map is stale. It runs the coverage gate twice, once with `--docs` and once with nothing excused, and proves the gate's own verdicts on three mutants of the built site. ADR 2026-10-31-1 carries an amendment for the mechanism.
