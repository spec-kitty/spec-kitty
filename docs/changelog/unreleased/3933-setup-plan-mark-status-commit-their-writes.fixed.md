- **`setup-plan` and `mark-status` commit the status writes they make** (#3933). **Before:** `setup-plan` committed `plan.md` alone and left the SpecifyCompleted, PlanStarted and PlanCompleted events it appended uncommitted, and `mark-status` committed its subtask annotation only on a single_branch Mission, leaving the status files modified in a coordination Mission's worktree. **After:** `setup-plan` commits the plan and its lifecycle events in one commit and reports that commit's hash (on a coordination Mission the lifecycle log is still left for a later commit), and `mark-status` commits its annotation on every topology when auto-commit is on; `--no-auto-commit` keeps the old, uncommitted behavior.
