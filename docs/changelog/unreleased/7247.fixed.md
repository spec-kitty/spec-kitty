- **`spec-kitty accept --diagnose` reports an uncomputable evidence check instead of failing on it, and a missing coordination worktree no longer crashes `accept`** (#7222, #7223). **Before:** a corrupt `lanes.json` made `accept --diagnose` exit 1 before the readiness report was built, and an unmaterialized coordination worktree crashed every `accept` mode with a raw traceback. **After:** `--diagnose` exits 0, lists `implementation_evidence skipped: <reason>` and leaves the blocker to the readiness report. Without `--diagnose`, `accept` exits 1 with a typed code: `EVIDENCE_WINDOW_UNCOMPUTABLE`, or `COORDINATION_WORKTREE_UNMATERIALIZED` with its `next_step`. **What to do:** nothing; for the coordination case run `spec-kitty doctor coordination --mission <slug> --fix`.
