- **`spec-kitty accept --diagnose` reports an evidence check it cannot compute instead of failing on it, and a missing coordination worktree no longer crashes `accept`** (#7222, #7223). **Before:** a corrupt `lanes.json` or an unreadable status log made `accept --diagnose` exit 1 before the readiness report was built, and an unmaterialized coordination worktree crashed every `accept` mode with a raw traceback. **After:** `--diagnose` exits 0, lists `implementation_evidence skipped: <reason>` and leaves the blocker to the readiness report. Otherwise `accept` exits 1 with a typed code: `EVIDENCE_WINDOW_UNCOMPUTABLE`, `COORDINATION_WORKTREE_UNMATERIALIZED` (with its `next_step`) or `EVIDENCE_STATUS_UNREADABLE`. **What to do:** nothing.
