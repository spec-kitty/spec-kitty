- **A leftover `kitty-specs/` folder no longer fakes a prefix collision on a healthy Mission** (#6757). **Before:** `doctor mission-state --audit` judged `DUPLICATE_PREFIX` and `AMBIGUOUS_SELECTOR` over every folder its own looser Mission rule accepted, so a folder holding only a `spec.md` — which Mission selection, `doctor identity` and `doctor mission-type` all treat as residue — made the engine report a collision on the real Mission that shares its numeric prefix. **After:** the two collision reports are judged on the Missions Mission selection can reach, so a folder no selector can reach can neither fake a collision nor silence one between two real Missions. Two real Missions sharing a prefix, and a pre-identity Mission whose tracked `meta.json` has no `mission_id` yet, are still reported exactly as before, and the residue folder keeps its own `IDENTITY_MISSING`, so `--fail-on error` still exits 1 until it is fixed or removed. **What to do:** nothing for a Mission whose report got shorter. A folder it still names as `IDENTITY_MISSING` needs a committed `meta.json` carrying a `mission_id`, or removal.
