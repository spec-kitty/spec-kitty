- **`upgrade` and `migrate backfill-identity` tell a stray `.git` apart from a repository, and name the real repair when Git cannot answer** (#7262, #7093, #7084, #7131, #7135). **Before:** any `.git` above a Mission folder blocked its identity repair with `TRACKED_META_UNAVAILABLE` and a `git checkout --` that could not work, while a repository that had lost its `HEAD` could be read as nothing tracked. **After:** an empty or hooks/locks-only `.git` is ignored and the identity is written. A repository, or a `.git` that is neither (a lost or dangling `HEAD`, other contents, an unreadable entry, a `GIT_DIR`-style override, or Git that cannot start), still refuses. The notice names the `.git` and the repair: fix the repository, or remove the residue. **What to do:** re-run a backfill that was refused only because of such residue.
