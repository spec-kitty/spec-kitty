- **More repository-root lookups honor `GIT_CEILING_DIRECTORIES` instead of adopting an enclosing checkout** (#7056, #7022). **Before:** checkout-identity, mission-metadata, legacy-charter, coordination, gitignore, env-file, ledger-posture, context, charter-lint, next-planner and catalog-diagnosis lookups could climb out of a hermetic tree. **After:** those parent-enumerating walks, the status read root, worktree-context probe and `regen` dev-checkout probe share one kernel-owned boundary. Unresolvable starts cannot escape through a ceiling alias; failed Git redirection, ownership or configuration probes refuse identity backfill rather than mint over an unknown tracked copy. The test suite's `tempfile` root sits inside its ceiling, child environments carry it, and the test-only `stop=` parameter is removed from `locate_project_root` and `find_repo_root` (#7076). Loop-form walks and the new root-walk gate remain tracked separately (#7269, #7122, #7056).
