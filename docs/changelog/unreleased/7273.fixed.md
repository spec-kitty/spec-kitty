- **Every repository-root walk stops at `GIT_CEILING_DIRECTORIES`, not just three of them** (#7056, #7022). **Before:** `locate_project_root`, `resolve_canonical_root` and `find_repo_root` honored the ceiling, but the checkout-identity, mission-metadata, legacy-charter, coordination, gitignore, env-file, ledger-posture, context, charter-lint, next-planner and catalog-diagnosis walks kept climbing — so inside a hermetic tree, mission identity, legacy bookkeeping, ledger reads and next-run planning could resolve into whatever checkout happened to enclose it. **After:** all of them (plus the status read root, the worktree-context probe and the `regen` dev-checkout probe) share one ceiling-aware ancestor walk at the `kernel` floor, beside `git_ceiling_dirs`, and an architectural gate rejects a new hand-rolled `[p, *p.parents]` walk outside it. The test suite's `tempfile` root now sits inside that ceiling too, from-scratch child environments carry it, and the test-only `stop=` parameter is gone from `locate_project_root` and `find_repo_root`, so the ceiling is the one boundary (#7076).
