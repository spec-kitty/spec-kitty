- **`spec-kitty consolidate` accepts an approval whose reviewer never pushed the review's own sync merge** (#7802). **Before:** the review claim merged the coordination branch into the lane in the reviewer's clone, the approval recorded that local merge as its approval stamp, and `consolidate` in any other clone refused `APPROVAL_STAMP_NOT_ON_LANE` with "the lane was rewritten". **After:** the approval also records `lane_head_presync`, the lane tip before that merge, and `consolidate` reads whichever recorded stamp the lane holds; a commit made after that tip still refuses `LANE_MOVED_AFTER_APPROVAL`. When neither stamp exists in the clone, the refusal says so and tells you to push the lane from the reviewer's clone, or to rework, review and approve again.
