- **The `move-task` help and the `spec-kitty-runtime-review` and `spec-kitty-implement-review` skills say a rejection without a review feedback file is refused** (#7677, #7716). **Before:** the skills said such a rejection is accepted locally but silently rejected by hosted sync, they and the `move-task` help offered a `--note` (or `--reason`) in place of the feedback file, and the `--review-feedback-file` help said omitting it made the status event contract-invalid. **After:** they say `move-task` refuses the rejection and records nothing, that a `--note` alone does not satisfy it, and that `--force` does not bypass it.
