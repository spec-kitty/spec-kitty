- **The `spec-kitty-runtime-review` and `spec-kitty-implement-review` skills say a rejection without a review feedback file is refused** (#7677, #7716). **Before:** they said such a rejection is accepted locally but silently rejected by hosted sync, and that a `--note` could stand in for the feedback file. **After:** they say `move-task` refuses the rejection and records nothing, that a `--note` alone does not satisfy it, and that `--force` does not bypass it.
