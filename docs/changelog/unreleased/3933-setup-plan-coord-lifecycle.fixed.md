- **`setup-plan` commits its lifecycle events on a coordination Mission** (#3933). **Before:** on a Mission created with `--topology coord` or `--topology lanes_with_coord`, `setup-plan` wrote `SpecifyCompleted`, `PlanStarted` and `PlanCompleted` to the coordination worktree's status log and left them uncommitted, so `doctor coordination` warned `COORDINATION_WORKTREE_DIRTY` until someone committed them by hand. **After:** they are committed on the coordination branch in the same run, and `--json` reports the commit as `lifecycle_commit`. If that commit is refused, `setup-plan` exits 1 with `SETUP_PLAN_LIFECYCLE_UNCOMMITTED` and names the events. Other Missions are unchanged.
