- **`spec-kitty migrate backfill-provenance`, `charter-encoding` and `rebaseline-dossier-hashes` now take a real `--mission`** (#6905, #6347). **Before:** since #5294 those three refused `--mission` outright, so an operator who legitimately wanted to re-run one migration against a single Mission had to run it corpus-wide. **After:** `--mission <handle>` (a `mission_id`, a `mid8` or a slug, resolved through the canonical mission selector as `migrate backfill-merge-commit` does) narrows the walk to that Mission's acceptance matrix, charter content or recorded dossier snapshot. `charter-encoding --mission` **skips the global charter store**, leaves it byte-for-byte unchanged and reports the exclusion in both the summary and `--json` (`mission`, `global_store_excluded`); the other two payloads gain `mission`. An unknown handle fails closed rather than widening the scope, omitting `--mission` keeps today's corpus-wide default, and `rewrite-opposed-by` / `repin-hooks` still reject the flag because they have no Mission concept.
