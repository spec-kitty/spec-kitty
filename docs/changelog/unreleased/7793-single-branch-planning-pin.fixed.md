- **A `single_branch` Mission keeps its committed work in review after a rebase** (#7793, #7297). **Before:** `finalize-tasks --refresh-planning-commit --allow-orphaned` re-pointed the planning commit to the branch tip, and `move-task --to for_review` then refused `SINGLE_BRANCH_IMPLEMENTATION_MISSING`. A minted `single_branch` Mission's refresh always refused `PLANNING_REFRESH_FAIL_CLOSED`. A second plain finalize replaced a derived pin. **After:** the re-pin follows the planning commit to its rebased copy (same patch-id) and refuses with `PLANNING_PIN_NO_PATCH_ID_MATCH`, `PLANNING_PIN_PATCH_ID_AMBIGUOUS` or `PLANNING_PIN_WOULD_EMPTY_WINDOW` instead of guessing. A `single_branch` pin lives on the mission branch. A derived pin is kept by a plain finalize (`planning_commit_derived` in `lanes.json`). `finalize-tasks`, `move-task` and `doctor mission-state --fix` name a working next step.
