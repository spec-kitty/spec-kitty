- **`spec-kitty implement` no longer refuses or commits the decisions lock** (#7493). **Before:** on a single_branch Mission that had used Decision Moments, `spec-kitty agent action implement` refused the claim with `WRITE_CHECKOUT_DIRTY` because of the untracked `kitty-specs/<mission>/decisions/index.json.lock`, and told you to commit it. On any topology, `spec-kitty implement`'s planning auto-commit committed that lock as a planning artifact. **After:** the claim check ignores every Mission's decisions lock, and the auto-commit never stages it; a legacy-tracked lock (modified or deleted) is likewise no longer a #1598 structural refusal or committed as a planning artifact. The lock stays untracked as intended (#5925, #6628).
