- **Two approved lanes that conflict now have a reviewed way to land** (#5604, #7754, #7755, #5721). **Before:** the stale-lane remedy told you to `git merge` the sibling lane's branch by hand, and `spec-kitty consolidate` then failed that merge only at the gate, after landing and rolling back. **After:** for an approved code lane the remedy names the sibling lane and the auto-rebase halt reason, and prints a re-review route: reopen the work package, `spec-kitty agent action implement <WP> --mission <mission> --incorporate <lane> --agent <agent>` (new: merges the sibling at its approval stamp; exits `3` on a conflict), resolve and commit, `move-task --to for_review`, an independent approval, then `consolidate`. Every printed `implement` line now carries `--agent`. A dependent stale lane first meets the dependency self-heal conflict; follow its message, then the route (follow-up #7880).
- **`spec-kitty consolidate` refuses an unreviewed conflict resolution before any branch moves** (#5604, #5721). **Before:** a merge commit on an approved lane that carried its own content was dropped at the gate after landing, or was not seen at all. **After:** the claim refuses with `RESOLUTION_MERGE_UNREVIEWED` when a merge with its own non-bookkeeping content is reached by no review approval of its lane (or lies in a canceled work package's window); a merge the auto-rebase recorded is skipped there only when its resolution kept one side's or the target's bytes (any other resolution is refused there too, since the gate would fail it), while the gate still judges it. The auto-rebase itself now refuses to merge under an unregistered merge attribute or driver in the lane checkout (for example an uncommitted `.gitattributes` with `merge=union`) and halts with the reason. `--dry-run` forecasts it, and `orchestrator-api consolidate-mission` reports it as `PREFLIGHT_FAILED` with contract version 1.31.0. When such a merge explains un-attributable content at the gate, the hint names re-review instead of `--dependency-ref`.
- **A rollback now undoes the run's own lane auto-rebase merges** (#7767). **Before:** a stale lane auto-rebased during a failed consolidation kept that merge commit after the rollback. **After:** the rollback restores the lane from its recorded, verified auto-rebase move under compare-and-swap; a lane with commits on top of that merge is kept and reported as `LANE_MOVED_SINCE_AUTO_REBASE`.
- **Merge probes refuse clone-local merge attributes and drivers** (#5604). **Before:** a `merge=union` in `.git/info/attributes`, an uncommitted `.gitattributes`, `core.attributesFile`, a custom merge driver or `merge.default` could make a hand resolution look like a clean merge to the gate. **After:** `consolidate` refuses to judge such a merge; only git's built-in merge, spec-kitty's registered drivers and a `merge=union` committed on the target branch count. A clone with `merge.renormalize=true` is refused by name; set `git config --local merge.renormalize false` and re-run.
- **The review context lists every merge in the review range** (#5604). **Before:** a merge whose combined diff left the owned files alone could be hidden from the reviewer. **After:** each merge gets a labeled `git show --remerge-diff` command line, or a line saying this git cannot show it.
- **A canceled lane brought into a re-approved dependency lane by a merge commit is named again** (#7497). **Before:** its content was refused under a generic un-attributable failure. **After:** a fresh run refuses with `CANCELED_REACHABLE_VIA_DEPENDENCY`.
- **The stale-lane reopen step works on a coordination Mission targeting protected `main`** (#7861). **Before:** `move-task --to planned --review-feedback-file` failed there. **After:** it commits its review evidence to the coordination branch. Follow-ups: #7864 (`move-task --note` on the same topology) and #7866 (recording `--attest-canceled-superseded` without starting a consolidation).
- **Signed commits no longer confuse the merge probes with `log.showSignature=true`** (#5604). **Before:** the signature check git prints before a `git show`/`git log` listing of a signed commit was read as a path (so a clean signed merge read as carrying its own content and was refused) or as the tree id the auto-rebase checks before recording a merge. **After:** those reads ignore signature, color and external-diff settings.
