- **`spec-kitty doctor coordination --fix` repairs only the Missions you name** (#7552). **Before:** `--fix` ran every repair (flattening `coordination_branch` keys, reverting stranded coordination `done` commits) across every Mission in the repository, `--mission` did not scope the stranded-revert repair, and a Mission whose `meta.json` exists only on its Mission branch (for example after its coordination worktree was removed) was not found at all. **After:** `--fix` needs `--mission <handle>` or `--all-missions` and otherwise refuses with `COORDINATION_FIX_SCOPE_REQUIRED` and changes nothing; `--mission` scopes every check and repair, stranded-revert markers included; and both the report and `--fix --mission` find a Mission that only its own Mission branch carries, so its missing coordination worktree can be materialized.
