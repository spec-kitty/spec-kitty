- **An approval records the reviewer who claimed the review** (#7569, #7573, #7571, #7570, #6709, #3451). **Before:** `move-task --to approved` recorded your `git config user.name` and an `auto-approval:` token, accepted any `--agent`, wrote a second review cycle when the reviewer had written one, and accepted a citation of a cycle that did not exist. **After:** every identity field names one reviewer: the asserted `--agent` (or `--reviewer`), else the review claimant. Another identity is refused with `APPROVER_NOT_CLAIMANT`; the reviewer's own `review-cycle-N.md` is adopted; a cited cycle must exist and be current (`REVIEW_CYCLE_CITATION_MISSING`); a later cycle that cannot be adopted refuses (`REVIEW_CYCLE_NOT_ADOPTABLE`). `approved -> done` copies the recorded approval, and a forced move records the forcing identity with `force-override` and writes no cycle.
