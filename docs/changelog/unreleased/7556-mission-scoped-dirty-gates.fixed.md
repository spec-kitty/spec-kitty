- **`record-analysis` and the single_branch `implement` claim no longer refuse on another Mission's files** (#7556). **Before:** an untracked file in another Mission's `kitty-specs/<mission>/` directory, or any other uncommitted file, made `record-analysis` refuse with `DIRTY_WORKTREE` and the single_branch claim refuse with `WRITE_CHECKOUT_DIRTY`, so agents stashed, excluded or moved those files. **After:** `record-analysis` refuses only on this Mission's own uncommitted paths and on the inputs its report fingerprints (such as the charter and `.kittify/config.yaml`), and reports the rest as a warning (`foreign_dirty_paths` in `--json`); the claim warns about another Mission's paths and still refuses on uncommitted code, which the work package would otherwise commit beside.
