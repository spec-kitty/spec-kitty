- **A bare implementer claim can be completed without a forced rewind, and a forced rewind no longer replaces the implementer of record** (#7712). **Before:** a claim recorded as the tool alone (`claude`) refused every same-tool approval, the `in_review -> for_review` re-submission that carried the implementer's profile was ignored, and a forced rewind into `in_progress` made its actor the implementer of record. **After:** that re-submission fills the missing profile or model (never contradicting a recorded one), a forced claim refines the implementer of record or is kept beside it, an approval must be independent of every identity that implemented the work package, and a profile-less claim warns at claim time.
