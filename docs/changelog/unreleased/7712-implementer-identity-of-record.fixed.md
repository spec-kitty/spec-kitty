- **An implementer can complete a profile-less claim, and a forced rewind no longer changes who implemented a work package** (#7712). **Before:** a claim recorded as the tool alone (`claude`) refused every same-tool approval until a forced rewind, and that rewind (or any forced or foreign claim) became the implementer of record. **After:** `move-task --to for_review --as-implementer --agent <tool>::<profile>` out of `in_review` fills the missing profile or model; a forced or foreign claim is kept beside the implementer of record and an approval must be independent of both; a profile-less claim warns at claim time.
