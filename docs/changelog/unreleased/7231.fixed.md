- **The advisory corpus suite no longer runs out of budget on unrelated PRs** (#7206). **Before:** `built-in / -m corpus suite (advisory)` ran at `timeout-minutes: 20` while its measured p95 was 20.3 min — 102 % of the cap — so four recent runs, including PRs whose diff touched no pack file, were killed at the budget with the suite at 97 % and no failing test, leaving a red advisory check landers attributed by hand. **After:** the job records its measured maximum suite wall-clock and takes its cap from that record with the existing #5378 headroom rule (`ceil((max-suite + 0:30) × 1.5)` = 31 min), so p95 is 65 % of the cap, and a run that still runs out of time reports INCOMPLETE/advisory through its notice instead of a bare cancellation (#4437). What the lane selects is unchanged; `tests/ci/test_nightly_timeout_headroom.py` and `tests/ci/test_corpus_suite_budget.py` hold both properties.
