2026-10-09 · claude-wp08 · WP08 Rule 1 whole-file design: a whole-file rewrite of a status/run log (write_text/write_bytes, open with 'w'/'r+', shutil.move/os.replace onto the log, atomic_write) is a finding UNLESS structurally safe. Four stated structural non-sinks: append-only open('a'); exclusive-create open('x') (cannot clobber an existing log — this is why the lane auto-rebase hydrate fix uses 'x'); the tmp-then-os.replace/shutil.move publish of a freshly-built file (source opened 'w'/'x' in-function — the engine _write_snapshot + rebuild_state); and a rewrite inside a lexical Mission-lock region (mission_write_lock/feature_status_lock/coord_status_lock — this is why the bookkeeping projection fix wraps its RMW in the lock). Targets are recognized by per-function dataflow over '<dir> / "<logfile>"' joins plus the events_path/events_file/EVENTS_FILENAME/STATE_FILE hints. The git merge driver is excluded by construction (it writes git's %A argv, a parameter, never a logfile-constant join) — a stated structural rule, not an allowlist entry. Rule 3 (A6): feature_status_lock keys accepted only as mission_lock_key(...) / a param / a name assigned from it; mission_write_lock & hold_mission_write_lock paths refuse a bare .name, a subscript, or a slug-named value. Allowlists stay empty; only the primitive module is excluded.

2026-10-09 · claude-wp15 · Rule 4 region recognition is structural, not name-based (A5): lexical with of a lock CM, with <name-from-cm>, ExitStack.enter_context(lock), manual __enter__/__exit__ span, and locked_acceptance_verdict_guard. Acceptance has three tiers: (1) sink lexically in a region; (2) callee is a self-locking named sink (restore_meta_text, detected structurally from its own body, so its cross-module callers in finalize_commit/bake are accepted without a region); (3) enclosing fn is 'covered' by a same-module call-site fixpoint (every call site in a region, recursively) — this is how the emit lock (feature_status_lock) and BookkeepingTransaction are recognised for the lane mirror two levels deep.
