# Design decisions

- **[2026-11-01][discovery] One parser for the selected-gates file.** The format is owned by `scripts/ci/battery_scope.py` (next to the writer the CI scope step calls); `battery_partition_plugin.read_gates_file` and the pre-step's `consumers` decision both parse through it, so the pre-step can never believe a gate is selected that the leg refuses to run.
- **[2026-11-01][discovery] Fail closed on an unreadable file, skip nothing silently.** A named gates file that is missing or unreadable exits non-zero with no answer, matching the plug-in's own fail-closed rule; the workflow step then reds the job rather than skipping a collection a selected gate may need.
