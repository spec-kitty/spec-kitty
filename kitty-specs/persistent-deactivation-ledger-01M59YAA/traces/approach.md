- **[2026-10-21][WP15] PR-body paragraph (paste into the PR).**

  > **Activation moves to the activation ledger.** From 4.0.0rc8, charter activation (`activated_<kind>`, `activated_kinds`, `mission_type_activations`) and the new `deactivated_<kind>` exclusions live in one git-tracked file, `.kittify/charter/activation.yaml` (ADR 2026-10-19-1, Amendment 2026-10-20). **For open branches:** after merging `main`, run `spec-kitty upgrade` (or the narrow `spec-kitty migrate activation-ledger`) in the repository root checkout and commit the result; merge the target into each lane, do not rebase. Activation keys that a branch from before this change writes into `charter.yaml` or `config.yaml` are ignored with a warning once the activation ledger exists, and `spec-kitty doctor charter-packs` reports them as `LEGACY_KEYS_PRESENT`; `migrate activation-ledger` strips a copy equal to the activation ledger and refuses one that differs. Deactivation needs the activation ledger: on a project that has not been upgraded, `charter deactivate` refuses and names `spec-kitty upgrade`, while reads stay on the old allow keys. Do not open an upgraded project with an older CLI: it reads every kind as unrestricted and ignores every deactivation.
